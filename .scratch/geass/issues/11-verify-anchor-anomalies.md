## Question

Do the PRD's named anchor cases actually exist as described — and if not, what real cases replace them?

The PRD leans on specific, quotable figures: SIRUP package `65174830` (Kab. Sukoharjo ceremonial flowers, IDR 200,000,000), `64154392` (Kelurahan Gayam *Sky View*, IDR 198,150,000), `65028950` (Sekretariat DPRD reception decorations, IDR 188,000,000), VIP open-house catering at IDR 3.2B, and unidentified "split IT procurement packages."

*SIRUP retrieval feasibility* established that these could not be confirmed — the PRD cites them with no retrieval method, and the domain it implies (`sirup.lkpp.go.id`) has been retired. Every one of them is therefore unverified, and a hero case that a judge can look up and find wrong is worse than no hero case at all.

Resolve, against the live SIRUP UI at `sirup.inaproc.id`:

- Does each `id_rup` exist, and does it resolve to the package the PRD describes?
- Does `total_pagu` match the quoted figure exactly?
- What do `nama_paket`, `uraian_pekerjaan`, `spesifikasi_pekerjaan`, and `volume_pekerjaan` actually contain — and is the "absurd" reading visible from the real text, or does it depend on a framing the data does not support?
- For the Sky View and DPRD cases in particular: is the ceremonial or recreational intent legible in the published fields, or only in the PRD's own prose?
- **Which of these five has a downstream LPSE tender?** A case that carries both a planning anomaly and a real execution trail can do the work of two cases in a three-minute video.
- Replacements: if any anchor is dead or misdescribed, find real substitutes from the same regions and budget years, and note what makes them defensible.

## Deliverable

A verified table: each anchor, whether it exists, its real figures versus the PRD's, and whether it is usable in the demo. Plus substitutes for anything that fails. Feeds directly into *The three-minute demo narrative*.
