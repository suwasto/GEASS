# GEASS

GEASS turns public procurement records into evidence-backed leads for human oversight. A lead is a reason to investigate, not a conclusion of wrongdoing.

## Language

**Planning package**:
A procurement plan published in SIRUP, identified by its RUP identifier and budget year. A plan is not proof that money was spent.
_Avoid_: Contract, transaction

**Tender**:
A procurement competition published by a particular LPSE portal. A tender identifier is meaningful with its originating portal.

**Lifecycle link**:
An evidenced connection between a planning package and an execution record. An absent link does not establish that a tender never happened.

**Vendor observation**:
A company name and identifiers as reported by a particular source. Similar names, shared premises, or masked tax identifiers do not establish common ownership.

**Benchmark**:
A price reference with a stated unit, specification, jurisdiction, effective period, and applicability. A retail price is not automatically a statutory ceiling.

**Risk signal**:
An observation that warrants review, supported by source evidence and stated limitations.
_Avoid_: Proven corruption, guilty vendor, confirmed cartel

**Finding**:
A reviewed assessment of one or more risk signals, including supporting evidence, counterevidence, and uncertainty.

**Insufficient evidence**:
An assessment that available records cannot support the proposed comparison or conclusion. It does not mean the package is normal.

**Dossier**:
A traceable evidence brief or draft information request assembled for a human to review and use.

**Publication**:
Making a reviewed finding visible to public users. Generating an assessment or dossier does not itself authorize publication.
