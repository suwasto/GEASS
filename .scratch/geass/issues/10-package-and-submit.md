## Question

What has to be true at the moment of submission, and getting it there?

The submission surface is not a formality: the competition requires all four artifacts and grades on them, so this ticket is the difference between a built prototype and a judged entry. It is the terminal ticket on the map — resolving it closes the destination.

## Checklist

1. **Public GitHub repository.** Created at submission time, per the team's charting decision. Contains no credentials, no service-account keys, no scraped bulk data that should not be republished.
2. **Deployed, publicly reachable link.** Cloud Run, confirmed reachable from outside the builder's network — and confirmed to still be reachable on submission day, since a demo that dies during judging scores zero.
3. **Demo video under three minutes.** Cut to the script from *The three-minute demo narrative*, and genuinely under the limit rather than near it.
4. **The deck.** Solution explanation, architecture, impact framing, mapped to the four weighted criteria.
5. **Submission form completed** with all four artifacts linked and the correct theme selected.
6. **Proof of build window.** Whatever demonstrates the work happened inside the 7 Sept – 18 Oct window, addressing the eligibility rule against pre-existing projects.
7. **Final integrity pass** against *Integrity and defamation stance*: names checked, claims checked, and nothing in the video or deck asserting more than the system does.

## Answer

Recorded on resolution: repository URL, deployed URL, video URL, and confirmation the submission was accepted.
