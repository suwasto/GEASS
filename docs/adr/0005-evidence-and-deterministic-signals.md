# Preserve evidence and abstain from unsupported comparisons

The user accepted evidence abstention and exact source links before fuzzy matching for the demo. Immutable source observations and explicit provenance take precedence over inferred facts. Prefer explicit source RUP links; fuzzy lifecycle matches remain candidates requiring review. Scope tender IDs by portal, preserve leading zeros in external identifiers, and distinguish absent fields from negative results.

Extract quantity and specification candidates with Gemini; validate them before deterministic decimal arithmetic. Do not divide a lump-sum `1 Paket` budget into a claimed comparable item price, compare unlike units, or treat a ceiling budget as actual spend. Match SSH references on jurisdiction, period, unit, specification, and applicability. Abstain when a defensible comparison is unavailable.

Store risk signals separately from the PRD's classification taxonomy so multiple signals and counterevidence can coexist. `NORMAL` requires sufficient evidence for the checks performed; `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` and `NOT_ASSESSED` are distinct. Procurement thresholds are effective-dated and category-specific, not a universal Rp200 million rule. Documents and descriptions are untrusted data: Model Armor screening complements schema validation, narrow tools, and prompt/data separation; it does not confer trust.
