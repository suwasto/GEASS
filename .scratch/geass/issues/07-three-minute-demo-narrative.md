## Question

What exactly happens on screen, in what order, to score against a rubric that weights Technical Merit & Gen AI Implementation at 40%, Problem Alignment & Impact at 25%, Innovation & Creativity at 25%, and UX & Solution Design at 10% — and which single real case carries it?

This ticket waits on the three retrieval tickets, because the narrative can only commit to cases and data the crawl has actually proven available. Deciding it earlier means scripting a demo around data that may not exist.

The PRD already drafts an arc. Test it rather than inherit it:

- **0:00–0:40** the problem: millions of SIRUP rows hiding ceremonial spend.
- **0:40–1:45** GEASS scans, flags `Absurd` and `Markup` rows with SSH grounding in IDR.
- **1:45–2:25** the Model Armor injection defense plus live Cloud Console traces.
- **2:25–3:00** one-tap PPID dossier generation.

Questions the draft leaves open:

- **The single anchor case.** One case has to carry the emotional weight. Which, from what the crawl proved retrievable — and does it have a real downstream LPSE tender, or does the graph beat have to switch cases mid-video?
- **Is the Model Armor beat worth 40 seconds?** It is a security checkbox that consumes a fifth of the video. Weigh it against more price-forensics time on the anchor case.
- **Where does the syndicate graph land?** It is the most differentiating idea in the PRD and the weakest data claim. If it is in, it needs a real tender and real disqualification reasons behind it.
- **What is the "wow"?** Name it explicitly. If there is no single moment a judge would repeat to a colleague, the video is not finished.
- **The Cloud Console proof shot.** Google hackathons expect visible proof of cloud usage. What exactly is shown, and is it legible at video resolution?
- **The close.** Guaranteed-impact framing in one sentence, tied to the 25% Impact criterion.

## Answer

A beat-by-beat script with timings, the named anchor case, and the one-line close.
